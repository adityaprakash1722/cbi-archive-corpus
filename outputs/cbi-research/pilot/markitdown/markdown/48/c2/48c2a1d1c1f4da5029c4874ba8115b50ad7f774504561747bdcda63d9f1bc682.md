Money and Banking Statistics – January 2017

There was a net increase of €131 million in deposits from households in January 2017, following on from a

€799 million increase in December 2016. The increases in these two months almost nullify the decrease of

€1 billion recorded in November 2016.

Loans  for  house  purchase  decreased  by  €153  million  in  net  terms  in  January,  a  decrease  of  1.2  per  cent

annually. Personal credit card expenditure in January 2017 was €78 million higher compared to the previous

year. Increased spending on services, especially in the transport sector, led to services accounting for the

largest share of expenditure.
